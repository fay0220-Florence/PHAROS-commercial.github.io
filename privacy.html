2. What we collect2. 我們收集什麼

  • Contact details you send us by email or WhatsApp: name, email address, phone number, company name and anything you choose to include in your message.聯絡資料：你透過電郵或 WhatsApp 提供的姓名、電郵、電話、公司名稱，以及你在訊息中自行提供的內容。
  • Transaction details when you buy from us: what was purchased, amount, currency, date and billing country. Card numbers are handled by Stripe, not by us.交易資料：購買項目、金額、幣種、日期與帳單國家。卡號由 Stripe 處理，本公司不會取得。
  • Technical data logged by our hosting provider when you visit the site: IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit.技術資料：你瀏覽網站時由託管服務商記錄的 IP 位址、瀏覽器類型、訪問頁面與時間。
  • Language preference stored locally in your browser so the site remembers your choice.語言偏好：儲存在你瀏覽器本機，用於記住你的語言選擇。

3. How we use it3. 用途

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the services or products you ask for.回覆你的查詢，並提供你所需的服務或產品。
  • To issue invoices, keep accounting records and meet tax and legal obligations.開立發票、保存帳目，以及履行稅務與法律責任。
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, and to enforce our Terms.防止欺詐與濫用，並執行本公司條款。
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.本公司不會出售個人資料，亦不會用於第三方廣告。

4. Payments4. 付款

Card payments are processed by Stripe. When you pay, Stripe collects and processes your payment details as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We receive only the information needed to confirm and reconcile the payment (for example amount, currency, last four digits and status). 卡付款由 Stripe 處理。你付款時，Stripe 以其自身私隱政策獨立處理你的付款資料；本公司只會收到確認與對帳所需的資料（例如金額、幣種、卡號末四位與交易狀態）。

5. Cookies and local storage5. Cookie 與本機儲存

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores a single language preference in your browser's local storage. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and performance. 本網站不使用廣告或分析 Cookie，只在你瀏覽器本機儲存一個語言偏好設定。託管服務商可能保留標準伺服器日誌，用於安全與效能用途。

6. Sharing6. 資料分享

  • Service providers who help us operate: hosting, email, payment processing (Stripe) and accounting.協助本公司營運的服務商：網站託管、電郵、支付處理（Stripe）與會計。
  • Professional advisers, courts or regulators where required by law.法律要求的專業顧問、法院或監管機構。
  • We do not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.本公司不會向廣告商或資料經紀分享個人資料。

7. Retention7. 保留期限

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 24 months.未成事的查詢：最多保留 24 個月。
  • Transaction and accounting records: 7 years, as required for Hong Kong tax purposes.交易與帳目紀錄：按香港稅務要求保留 7 年。
  • Server logs: as kept by our hosting provider, normally a short period.伺服器日誌：按託管服務商的常規，一般為短期。

8. Your rights8. 你的權利

Under the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, you may request access to, and correction of, the personal data we hold about you. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data of Hong Kong. 根據《個人資料（私隱）條例》，你有權查閱及更正本公司持有的你的個人資料。請電郵我們，我們會在合理時間內回覆。如你不滿意，可向香港個人資料私隱專員公署投訴。

9. Security9. 保安

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. 本公司採取合理的技術與管理措施保護個人資料，包括加密連線（HTTPS）與存取控制。惟任何傳輸或儲存方式均無法保證絕對安全。

10. Changes10. 更新
