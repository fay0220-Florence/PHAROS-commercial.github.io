SnowDrift

Your social media data companion. One place for trends and personal stats across Bilibili, Douyin, Weibo and Xiaohongshu. 你的社交媒體數據管家。一個介面看清 Bilibili、抖音、微博、小紅書的熱門趨勢與個人數據。

What it does功能

Less switching between apps, more seeing what is actually happening. 少一點切換 App，多一點看清真正發生什麼。

Multi-platform aggregation多平台聚合

Trends and personal stats from Bilibili, Douyin, Weibo and Xiaohongshu in a single view. Bilibili、抖音、微博、小紅書的熱門趨勢與個人數據，集中在一個畫面。

Local discovery地理探索

An LBS heat map for finding what is trending around you. LBS 熱力圖，找出你身邊正在流行的內容。

Smart favourites智能收藏

Cross-platform saved posts and topics, with tags and search. 跨平台收藏帖子與話題，支援標籤與搜尋。

Realtime monitoring實時監控

Follow a topic as it climbs, without refreshing five apps. 話題升溫時即時看到，不用開五個 App 輪流刷新。

Deeper analysis深度分析

Charts that show why a post moved, not just that it did. 用圖表看清一篇內容為什麼爆，而不只是爆了。

Works on your devices跨裝置使用

iOS, Android and the web — add it to your home screen for a native feel. iOS、Android 與網頁；可加入主畫面，體驗接近原生 App。
